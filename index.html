Our foundation

Why we do this

Building strong, creative and innovative leadership minds in Kibera.

Our mission

To nurture raw talent through art, dance, and education—giving every child the tools to lead and inspire.

Our vision

A platform where young talents thrive, positively impacting their community and creating a better tomorrow.

Our impact

Serving 200+ youth weekly with free creative programs, daily meals, and essential mental health support.

Our objectives

How we put the mission into practice

Five commitments that guide every session, performance and conversation at the centre.

01

Create an enabling platform through which young talents showcase their abilities to the world.

02

Enable young talents to acquire skills in abilities of their own choice through dedicated nurturing.

03

Establish a forum that creates a network and effective communication where young talents market their abilities.

04

Remain a mentor and a good role model to our children in the community, building a better society.

05

Be at the frontline of change in our community, leading with creativity, compassion and purpose.

What we do

Four free programs, one goal

Give every child room to grow, whatever their starting point.

Art and painting

School-holiday and after-school workshops where children paint, draw and build a portfolio of their own work.

Dance, music and drama

Performance mentorship across genres, staged for real audiences at festivals and concerts.

Daily meals

A nutritious meal every day a child walks through our doors. No child creates on an empty stomach.

Mental health support

Creative expression as healing: a safe space to process, talk and be heard.

Dance academy

Discipline in the afternoons. Healing on the stage.

Transforming the community through dance, one session at a time.

Building all-round dancers

Our objective is simple: transform the community through dance, and turn the stories told in movement into healing, building an all-round dancer ready for the dance industry.

Our dancers have performed at the Miondoko festival, the Artolution and AfroCure concert, the Auto-Pilot album launch and showground auditions, as a government-certified centre.
