Programs

Art & Painting

Where imagination meets canvas. Our art program gives children in Kibera the tools, space, and mentorship to express their stories, build portfolios, and discover the joy of visual creation.

Our Approach

Creative Expression as a Tool for Growth

Every child has a story to tell. Through painting, drawing, and mixed media, we help our students translate their experiences, dreams, and observations into powerful visual narratives.

We provide all necessary materials—canvases, brushes, paints, and sketchbooks—ensuring that financial barriers never stand in the way of a child's creative potential. Our workshops are led by experienced local artists who serve as mentors and role models.

What We Offer

More Than Just a Paintbrush

Our curriculum is designed to build technical skills while nurturing confidence, focus, and a sense of community.

Guided Workshops

Structured sessions where students learn foundational techniques in color theory, composition, and perspective.

Portfolio Building

Older students are guided in curating a personal portfolio, preparing them for secondary education and creative careers.

Exhibitions & Murals

We showcase student work at local events and create large-scale community murals that beautify Kibera.
