Our Programs

Programs

Five free programs, one goal: give every child room to grow through creativity, nourishment and support.

What we offer

Open to every child, at no cost

Each program runs throughout the week at the centre in Kibera and is free for all participants.

Art and painting

School-holiday and after-school workshops where children paint, draw and build a portfolio. Participants explore different mediums, learn colour theory and express their world through visual art. Every piece tells a story of Kibera.

Dance, music and drama

Performance mentorship across genres, staged for real audiences at festivals and concerts. From Afrobeat to contemporary, hip hop to African traditional, our young performers learn discipline, teamwork and the power of storytelling through movement.

Daily meals

A nutritious meal every day a child walks through our doors. We believe creativity flourishes when basic needs are met, so our meal program ensures every child has the energy to learn, create and grow.

Mental health support

Creative expression as healing. Through art therapy, group discussions and one-on-one mentorship, we help children navigate challenges and build emotional resilience in a safe, consistent space.

Community service

Children give back through regular community projects — neighbourhood clean-ups, visits to elders, tree planting and peer mentorship. Service builds empathy, responsibility and a sense of belonging, turning young participants into active contributors to Kibera's future.

How a week works

A steady routine children can rely on

Consistency is part of the program. Children know what to expect, and who will be there.

Afternoons

Art, music and drama sessions run after school, with materials provided and mentors on hand throughout.

Daily meals

Every child receives a meal on the days they attend, served before or between sessions.

Mentorship

Older students and staff mentor younger participants, building confidence and shared responsibility.

Help keep these programs free

Every session, meal and mentoring hour is funded by people who believe in Kibera's children.
