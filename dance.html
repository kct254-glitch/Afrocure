Dance Academy

Discipline in the afternoons. Healing on the stage. Transforming Kibera through movement.

Our approach

Building all-round dancers for the industry

Our objective is simple: transform the community through dance, and turn the stories told in movement into healing, building an all-round dancer ready for the dance industry.

Weekly training runs daily, instilling discipline and teamwork in every session. Our dancers have performed at the Miondoko festival, the Artolution and AfroCure concert, the Auto-Pilot album launch and showground auditions.
