About AfroCure

Creativity. Community. Care. Since 2018 in the heart of Kibera, Nairobi.

Our story

A small gathering that became a centre

Founded in 2018 by PINTO (Amos Odhiambo), AfroCure Arts and Talent Centre was born from a simple belief: every child in Kibera deserves access to creativity, nourishment and mentorship.

What started as a small gathering of children with paintbrushes has grown into a thriving centre serving over 200 children and youth every week, offering free art workshops, dance training, daily meals and mental health support.

In 2023 we launched our Dance Academy, expanding our reach and deepening our impact. Today AfroCure stands as a government-certified centre and a steady presence for families in Kibera.

Our motto: "Art for growth, voices for change."

What guides us

Mission and vision

Mission

To build strong, creative and innovative leadership minds by nurturing talent through art, dance, music and education.

Vision

To provide a platform for young talents to thrive and have a positive impact on their community, creating a better society of tomorrow.

Our objectives

How we put the mission into practice

01

Create an enabling platform through which young talents showcase their abilities.

02

Enable young talents to acquire skills through dedicated nurturing of their talent.

03

Establish a forum for networking and marketing young talents' abilities.

04

Remain a mentor and role model to build a better society of tomorrow.

05

Be at the frontline of change in our community.
